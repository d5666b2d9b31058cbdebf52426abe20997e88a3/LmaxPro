Lamx-Pro

Privacy Policy · Ember Terrace mini-game collection

Lamx-Pro is a casual game collection inspired by kiln yards and ember terraces. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what Lamx-Pro does and does not do with your information.

1. Summary

Lamx-Pro is designed for play on your device. We do not collect personal information, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any profile details.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Lamx-Pro does not gather, purchase, or receive:

  • Personal identifiers
  • Contact details
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics sent to third parties

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To improve your play experience, Lamx-Pro may save small amounts of data locally on your iPhone or iPad, including:

  • Best scores and progress marks for each mini-game
  • Preferences such as haptics and sound on or off
  • Whether you have seen in-game guidance for certain modes

This information stays on your device. You can remove saved marks from Kiln Bench in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

4. Games Included

Lamx-Pro offers six skill-based mini-games on the Ember Terrace hub:

  • Klondike Stack — classic solitaire stacking
  • One Stroke — trace every tile in one path
  • Four Pick — choose the correct answer
  • Path Block — place barriers to guide routes
  • Sync Switch — switch lanes on the beat
  • Paint Fill — cover the yard before rivals cut your trail

Quiz content in Four Pick is bundled with the app. No personal data is needed to play any mode.

5. Children’s Privacy

Lamx-Pro is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe we have received personal information in error, please contact us so we can assist with removal steps on your device.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties because we do not collect it. Lamx-Pro does not include social features, purchases tied to identity, or gambling mechanics.

7. Security

Because data remains on your device, you control it through your device settings and by clearing saved marks inside the app. We recommend keeping your device passcode enabled.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when Lamx-Pro is updated. The revised version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or Lamx-Pro, please email us at Lamx-Pro@outlook.com.
