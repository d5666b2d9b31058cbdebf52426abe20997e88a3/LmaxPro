Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Overview

LmaxPro is a casual game collection built for quick, relaxed play on your iPhone or iPad. We designed the app to keep things simple: your gameplay stays on your device, and you stay in control.

Information Stored on Your Device

LmaxPro may store the following data locally to support gameplay and preferences:

  • Game progress, best scores, stars, and recently played modes
  • Tutorial completion flags for each mini game
  • Settings choices such as haptics and sound feedback

This information is saved only on the device where you use the app. It is used to resume progress, show personal bests, and remember your preferred feedback settings.

What We Do Not Collect

LmaxPro does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal profile details. We do not use advertising identifiers for tracking, and we do not sell personal information.

Children’s Privacy

LmaxPro is suitable for broad audiences. The app does not request personal details from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about how the app stores progress on a shared family device, you can remove saved data at any time from Settings.

Your Choices

You can manage your experience inside the app:

  • Turn haptics or sound on or off in Settings
  • Reset local progress with the “Reset Local Progress” option in Settings
  • Remove all app data by deleting LmaxPro from your device

Third-Party Services

LmaxPro is a self-contained entertainment app. It does not integrate third-party analytics, social features, or payment modules that collect personal information on our behalf.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the effective date shown at the top of this page. Continued use of LmaxPro after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how LmaxPro handles data on your device, please contact us at: support@lmaxpro.app
